Technology

From halogen limitation to bi-LED precision

Traditional halogen bulbs waste energy as heat and produce a warm, limited beam that struggles on unlit roads and in poor weather. Modern LED and bi-LED systems deliver higher luminous efficacy, a cooler white colour temperature that improves contrast, and optical control that halogen simply cannot match.

Our bi-LED projectors integrate high and low beam into a single lens with a sharp cutoff. The result is a wider, more even spread of light on the road surface and a defined upper edge that protects other drivers. Whether you choose a plug-and-play Core bulb or a full Matrix assembly, the goal is the same: clarity, safety and a refined presence after dark.

Thermal design matters as much as brightness. Copper-core cooling, properly rated drivers and stable voltage supply keep output consistent over thousands of hours — which is why every Light Master product carries a two-year replacement commitment.

Questions

Frequently asked

Will LED bulbs work in my halogen housing?

In many cases yes. Compatibility depends on bulb type, housing design and the vehicle’s electrical system. A correctly chosen LED can improve brightness significantly, but the beam only stays safe and legal with proper alignment. Send your model and year on WhatsApp and we will advise the right product and any fitment notes.

Do you ship outside India?

Yes. We serve Oman, the United Arab Emirates and Qatar in addition to deliveries across India. Message us with your city and the products you need for delivery options and timing.

What does the 2-year warranty cover?

Manufacturing faults in bulbs, drivers and assemblies for two years from the invoice date. Physical damage, water ingress from incorrect fitting, and third-party modifications are not covered. Claims are handled via WhatsApp with your invoice details.

Can workshops buy at trade prices?

Yes. We support garages, detailing studios and installers with trade pricing and stock reliability. Contact us on WhatsApp with your business details to discuss terms.
