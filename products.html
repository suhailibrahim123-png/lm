Guidance

Headlight bulbs that earn their place

Upgrading from halogen to LED is one of the highest-impact changes you can make to night-time safety and confidence. Light Master headlight bulbs are selected for output quality, colour consistency and thermal stability — not merely peak lumen claims on a box.

Our Core range delivers a clean 6000K white beam with plug-and-play convenience. The Pro series adds copper-core cooling and tighter beam focus for drivers who demand more from unlit roads. Apex Canbus units are engineered for platforms that are sensitive to electrical load — particularly many German and Korean vehicles — so you avoid dashboard errors and flickering.

If you are evaluating a full bi-LED projector conversion, we will discuss housing depth, lens diameter and alignment procedure before you commit. The goal is a result that looks intentional and performs safely — not a bright but uncontrolled scatter of light.
